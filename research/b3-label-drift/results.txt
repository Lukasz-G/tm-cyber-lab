WEAK ASSOCIATION at month resolution: real but small. Reportable as a caveat on any
claim that treats the labels as fixed, not as a result in itself.
The control holds: feature drift measured on unambiguously-benign samples tracks the
all-sample series, so that series is not an artifact of the labels reshaping the
population being averaged over.

under-specified: it tested a correlation threshold without requiring significance, which at
eight transitions it cannot have. The monthly result above supersedes it.
CONFOUNDED: label fragility and feature drift move together on this dataset, so a result
that claims to separate them is not supported here.
